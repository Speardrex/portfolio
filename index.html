CLINICAL HEALTHCARE SYSTEMS · TRIAGE EFFICIENCY

Emergency Room Patient Flow

Why are six out of ten patients waiting too long?

Emergency rooms run on minutes, but this one was losing them. I mapped 19 months of clinical data to prove that chronic wait times weren't caused by a constant flood of patients, but by predictable, localized bottlenecks.

01 The Problem

How can emergency-room visit data help reveal patterns in waiting times and patient flow? Average waiting time alone cannot explain how demand, admissions, referrals, and patient experience vary across the reporting period.

02 The Approach

Built a Power BI report with a data model and DAX measures to examine waiting times against a 30-minute analytical benchmark, explore patient and referral patterns, and compare activity across days, hours, and months.

03 The Finding

The report recorded an average waiting time of 35.3 minutes, above the selected 30-minute benchmark. It also revealed variations in visit volume across days and hours, while substantial missingness in satisfaction responses limited how confidently patient experience could be interpreted.
